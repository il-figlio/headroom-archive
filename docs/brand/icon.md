# Headroom icon

A white gauge on a flat black tile shows its needle near the low end. The unused sweep represents available memory and storage capacity. The arc and needle stay distinct at menu bar sizes.

The built-in image generation tool produced the master at `Resources/Headroom.png`. Run `./scripts/build-icon.sh` to generate the macOS icon bundle at `Resources/Headroom.icns`.

## Generation prompt

Create one production macOS app icon for Headroom, a status utility showing available memory and disk capacity. Use the instantly recognizable silhouette of a simple instrument gauge with the needle pointing toward the low end of the scale, indicating plenty of headroom. Construct the gauge from exactly two solid white graphic forms: a broad smooth semicircular arc running from the left at 9 o'clock over the top to the right at 3 o'clock, with square flat ends, and a thick white diagonal needle pointing from the center of the circle toward 10 o'clock and ending clearly before the arc. The arc is open underneath. Needle angled up-left, simple straight stroke with a small round base integrated into the same shape. No tick marks, no lettering, no underside line. Center the two shapes as a group by their visible bounds, gauge filling roughly 60% of the tile width. Make the arc and needle thick enough to be clear at 16 px. Use pure white on a flat black continuous-corner macOS rounded-square tile. Premium ultra-minimal developer-tool aesthetic, precise geometry, zero texture, no depth, no embossing, no gradients, no shadows, no accent colors, no second symbol, no frame around the gauge. The tile is centered and takes up roughly 83% of a square canvas with genuine transparent alpha outside it. Render exactly one front-on icon, no mockup.
